# Companion roster

Each companion has a colony specialty, a disposition axis for relationship choices, and three trust gates. Gate labels are empty placeholders in `game/bonds.rpy`; the names below are stable scene IDs, not authored scenes. Personal trust is tracked separately from faction standing.

| Companion | Archetype | Colony role | Disposition axis | Trust gates / scene labels |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mara | Field medic | Medical care and triage | Candor ↔ discretion | 1 `bond_scene_mara_gate_1`; 2 `bond_scene_mara_gate_2`; 3 `bond_scene_mara_gate_3` |
| Sable | Salvage scout | Scouting and salvage runs | Caution ↔ boldness | 1 `bond_scene_sable_gate_1`; 2 `bond_scene_sable_gate_2`; 3 `bond_scene_sable_gate_3` |

| Ren | Grid technician | Power systems and repairs | Improvisation ↔ procedure | 1 `bond_scene_ren_gate_1`; 2 `bond_scene_ren_gate_2`; 3 `bond_scene_ren_gate_3` |
| Iona | Water engineer | Water recovery and distribution | Self-reliance ↔ cooperation | 1 `bond_scene_iona_gate_1`; 2 `bond_scene_iona_gate_2`; 3 `bond_scene_iona_gate_3` |
